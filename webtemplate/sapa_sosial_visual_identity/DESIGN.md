## Brand & Style

### Brand Personality & Philosophy
The portal serves as the primary civic gateway for social welfare services provided by Dinas Sosial Kabupaten Blitar. The brand personality is grounded in three core principles:
- **Trustworthy & Authoritative (Kredibel & Tertib):** Instills immediate civic confidence. The visual tone must feel legitimate, secure, and officially backed by the regional government without appearing bureaucratic, cold, or intimidating.
- **Warm & Compassionate (Peduli & Inklusif):** Serves vulnerable demographics—families seeking assistance (DTSEN, PIP, KIS/PBI-JK), elderly individuals, and citizens in crisis. The interface communicates warmth, safety, and human dignity.
- **Radically Accessible & Clear (Terbuka & Mudah Dipahami):** Built mobile-first for citizens using entry-to-mid-range Android smartphones across rural and urban districts of Kabupaten Blitar. Every element guarantees immediate legibility, unambiguous language, and zero cognitive friction.

### Design Movement
The visual language operates under a **Clean Modern Civic / Modern Institutional** style. It combines structured civic reliability (clear content hierarchy, explicit status signaling, high-contrast borders) with soft modern digital touchpoints (generous card padding, rounded geometry, subtle atmospheric shadows, and welcoming open typography). It rejects visual clutter, unneeded skeuomorphic gradients, and non-functional decorative motifs in favor of clean lines, Lucide-style functional line iconography, and large, finger-friendly interactive zones.

## Colors

### Color Strategy & Hierarchy
- **Primary Teal (`#0F766E`):** Represents government stability, institutional authority, health, and social welfare security. Applied across main headers, brand marks, primary service identifiers, and standard navigational controls.
- **Secondary Warm Amber (`#F59E0B`):** Acts as the high-priority action trigger. Dedicated strictly to critical citizen conversion points (such as primary application CTAs "Ajukan Layanan" and form submission buttons) to guide users intuitively toward key tasks.
- **Off-White Canvas (`#F8FAFC`):** Soft, neutral backdrop designed to reduce screen glare on outdoor mobile screens while providing high contrast against pure white component cards.
- **Card Surfaces (`#FFFFFF`):** Crisp pure white panels that isolate content into scannable chunks.

### Accessible Contrast (WCAG AA Compliance)
All text colors paired against background fills strictly meet or exceed the 4.5:1 contrast ratio for normal text and 3:1 for large text:
- Dark slate text (`#0F172A`) against `#FFFFFF` and `#F8FAFC` provides an optimal contrast ratio of 16.1:1.
- Primary Teal (`#0F766E`) against pure white delivers 5.4:1 contrast.
- Secondary Amber (`#F59E0B`) uses dark slate text (`#0F172A`) for button labels to maintain a compliant 7.2:1 contrast ratio.
- Inline status tags (e.g., "Surat Terbit", "Perlu Perbaikan", "Diproses") utilize saturated text hues paired with 10% tinted pastel backgrounds for immediate cognitive recognition without visual vibration.

## Typography

### Typographic Rationale
The entire portal employs **Plus Jakarta Sans**, a modern geometric grotesque font engineered specifically for clean digital rendering, balanced vertical metrics, and distinct counters. Its soft yet structural shapes offer an inviting, approachable human character suitable for civic and social services.

### Scale, Legibility, and Sizing Rules
- **Minimum 16px for Body Text:** In accordance with accessibility requirements for citizens with varying visual acuity and digital literacy, standard running text and primary input labels default to at least 16px (`body-lg` or `label-lg`).
- **Headings & Hierarchy:** Hero headlines use strong structural weights (700 and 800) with slight negative tracking (`-0.02em`) to bind headline phrases together. Mobile variants clamp down gracefully to prevent awkward word wraps on narrow displays (360px–412px).
- **Line Heights:** Generous line heights (`1.5` to `1.6`) ensure long bureaucratic explanations, multi-step directions, and requirements checklists remain strain-free to read.

## Layout & Spacing

### Grid Model & Breakpoints
The layout follows an accessible, mobile-first responsive architecture:
- **Mobile (`< 640px`):** 4 columns, `16px` (`1rem`) outer margins, `16px` gutters. Content runs as full-width stacked modules with vertical flow. Sticky bottom CTA bars pin key actions above the thumb zone.
- **Tablet (`640px – 1023px`):** 8 columns, `24px` (`1.5rem`) outer margins, `20px` gutters. Form fields shift into 2-column groupings where contextually linked (e.g., Kecamatan and Desa).
- **Desktop (`>= 1024px`):** 12 columns, max-width constrained to `1200px` (`max-w-7xl` or `max-w-6xl` for focused form pages), centered canvas margins, `24px` gutters. Split views allow persistent sidebars for ticket trackers, contextual guides, and contact support.

### Spacing Rhythm & Touch Targets
- **44px Minimum Touch Target:** All interactive controls (buttons, input fields, selects, pagination links, and accordion headers) enforce a vertical minimum height of 44px (with 48px to 52px preferred for primary mobile buttons) to accommodate one-handed thumb interaction.
- **Rhythm Scale:** Spacing follows an 8px base rhythm (`0.5rem`, `1rem`, `1.5rem`, `2rem`, `3rem`). Interior card padding defaults to `space-lg` (`24px`) on desktop and scales to `space-md` (`16px`) on mobile devices.

## Elevation & Depth

### Ambient Soft Elevation Architecture
Depth is communicated through low-opacity ambient drop shadows paired with crisp border outlines (`#E2E8F0`), preventing heavy artificial drops and ensuring crisp presentation across both OLED and budget LCD mobile panels:

- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** Applied to the base canvas (`#F8FAFC`). No shadow.
- **Level 1 (Card Rest / Interactive Tiles):** Applied to standard information cards, form containers, and ticket search modules.
  `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04); border: 1px solid #E2E8F0;`
- **Level 2 (Hover / Elevated Card / Dropdown):** Applied when hovering over service cards or when toggling active dropdowns.
  `box-shadow: 0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 118, 110, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 118, 110, 0.04); border: 1px solid #CBD5E1;`
- **Level 3 (Sticky Nav / Modal / Bottom Sheet):** Applied to the fixed top navigation bar, sticky mobile CTA bars, and full modal dialogs.
  `box-shadow: 0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04);`

Subtle teal-tinted shadows are used exclusively on active cards to tie the elevation cues back to the primary civic brand palette.

## Shapes

### Rounded Geometry Hierarchy
With a base roundedness token of `2` (`0.5rem` / `8px`), the system scales proportional corner radii to match element prominence:

- **Buttons, Text Inputs, and Dropdowns:** `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / `8px`). Balances tactile friendliness with structured input form efficiency.
- **Cards, Modals, and Main Panels:** `rounded-2xl` (`1rem` / `16px`). Gives civic cards a soft, modern, and friendly frame that separates distinct content sections cleanly.
- **Chips, Category Tags, and Status Badges:** `rounded-full` (pill shape). Visually distinguishes metadata tags and selectable filters from actionable rectangular buttons.
- **Drag-and-Drop File Upload Zones:** `rounded-xl` (`0.75rem` / `12px`) with a `2px dashed #CBD5E1` border stroke.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary Action (Warm Amber CTA):** Background `#F59E0B`, text `#0F172A`, font-weight 700, min-height 48px, rounded 8px (`rounded-lg`), horizontal padding 24px (`px-6`). Hover state shifts to `#D97706`. Focused with an outline ring of `3px solid rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.4)`. Reserved for critical user conversion triggers like "Ajukan Layanan", "Lacak Status", and "Kirim Pengajuan".
- **Secondary Civic (Teal):** Background `#0F766E`, text `#FFFFFF`, font-weight 600, min-height 48px, rounded 8px. Hover shifts to `#115E59`.
- **Outline / Ghost:** Border `1.5px solid #0F766E`, background transparent, text `#0F766E`, font-weight 600, min-height 48px. Used for secondary actions such as "Sampaikan Pengaduan" and "Kembali ke Beranda".
- **Mobile Sticky Action Bar:** Fixed bottom container (`bg-white/95 backdrop-blur-md border-t border-slate-200 p-4`) containing full-width button triggers for complex multi-step application flows.

### 2. Form Inputs & Field Controls
- **Text Inputs & Dropdowns:** Min-height 48px, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #CBD5E1`, border-radius 8px, text size 16px (`text-slate-900`), placeholder `#94A3B8`. Active focus triggers border `#0F766E` with `ring-3 ring-teal-700/15`.
- **Error State:** Border `#DC2626`, focus ring `#DC2626/15`, with an explicit error label positioned directly below the field in 14px text alongside a red alert circle icon.
- **Radio Selection Cards:** Large interactive cards containing radio buttons, bold title, and contextual description (e.g., purpose of DTSEN or KIS reactivation reasons). Border `1.5px solid #E2E8F0`; selected state transforms to border `#0F766E`, background `#F0FDFA`, with a filled teal radio indicator.

### 3. Cards & Information Panels
- **Service & Quick Action Cards:** Crisp white background (`#FFFFFF`), rounded 16px (`rounded-2xl`), border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, padding 24px. Features a 48x48px rounded-xl icon container with soft teal background (`#CCFBF1`) and teal icon (`#0F766E`).
- **Ticket Tracking Quick Card:** Elevated card featuring dual input fields for "Nomor Tiket" (placeholder: `DTSEN-202610-00012`) and "4 Digit Terakhir NIK / No. HP", paired with an inline high-contrast submit button.

### 4. Chips, Status Badges & Category Filters
- **Filter Chips:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), min-height 38px, padding 8px 16px. Unselected: background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, text `#475569`. Selected: background `#0F766E`, border `1px solid #0F766E`, text `#FFFFFF`.
- **Status Badges:** Small pill badges (`rounded-full py-1 px-3 text-xs font-bold`):
  - *Diproses / Menunggu:* `#FEF3C7` background with `#B45309` text.
  - *Selesai / Terbit / Valid:* `#DCFCE7` background with `#15803D` text.
  - *Perlu Perbaikan / Ditolak:* `#FEE2E2` background with `#B91C1C` text.
  - *Pemeriksaan Berkas:* `#E0F2FE` background with `#0369A1` text.

### 5. Multi-Step Form Stepper & Timelines
- **Horizontal Stepper (Desktop):** Step counter circles (36x36px) joined by 2px horizontal rules. Active step features a teal filled circle with bold white text; completed steps display a green circle with a check icon; upcoming steps are outlined in muted slate.
- **Vertical Progress Timeline (Tracking Result):** Used on status tracking views. Left vertical rule connects state nodes. Active step features a teal node with an animated pinging outer ring, accompanied by step title, date, time, and formal officer notes ("Catatan Petugas").

### 6. File Upload Zone
- **Drag-and-Drop Area:** Min-height 140px, rounded 12px, border `2px dashed #CBD5E1`, background `#F8FAFC`. Contains upload cloud icon, bold label ("Pilih file atau seret ke sini"), supported format badge ("PDF, JPG, PNG maks 2MB"), and file item preview cards equipped with remove buttons and upload progress bars.
